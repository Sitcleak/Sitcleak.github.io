Ytb Shorts

Learn real English from your favorite sitcoms. One expression a day.

Ytb Shorts is the publishing tool of an English-learning YouTube channel. Each Short shows one complete sitcom scene with subtitles, highlights one everyday expression, and explains it in the description with a short quiz in the comments.

What this tool does

  • Uploads the channel's own Shorts to its YouTube account and schedules their publication date, using YouTube API Services.
  • Posts one quiz comment, written by the channel, under each published Short.

It is a private tool: it runs on the channel owner's computer, and the channel owner is its only user. It does not access any other YouTube account and collects no data from viewers.
