Privacy Policy

Ytb Shorts · Last updated: November 26, 2026

Who uses this tool

Ytb Shorts is a private tool that runs on the computer of the owner of one YouTube channel. The channel owner is its only user. It is not offered to the public.

YouTube API Services

Ytb Shorts uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy also applies.

What data it accesses and why

With the channel owner's permission (Google sign-in), Ytb Shorts accesses that YouTube account only to:

  • upload the channel's own videos and set their title, description and publication date;
  • post one comment under each of the channel's own videos.

It does not read, collect, store or share any data about viewers or other YouTube users.

Storage and sharing

The Google authorization token is stored only on the channel owner's computer. No data is sent to any server other than Google's, and no data is sold or shared with third parties.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account's security settings: myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the channel's YouTube page (About section).
